# Falsification strategy

| Claim | Strongest alternative | Decisive test | Falsified when |
|---|---|---|---|
| Residual program is induced | Rare pretreatment cells were missed | Depth-matched binomial/downsampling detection model | Pretreatment absence is compatible with observed depth in most patients |
| Change occurs within clone | Different clone/region was sampled | scDNA where available; conservative CNA matching; region sensitivity | Signal exists only across unmatched clone/site |
| Program is resistance-related | Generic stress or arrest | Matched stress, hypoxia, apoptosis and cell-cycle covariates; HR+ control | Effect disappears or is equally strong in generic treatment stress |
| Program generalizes | Study-specific batch | Locked scoring in independent platform/institution | Direction fails or reverses externally |
| Niche constrains state | Shared local hypoxia | Spatially restricted nulls and covariate adjustment | Neighborhood association vanishes under spatial null |

Negative results remain first-class outputs. No validation cohort may be replaced post hoc merely because it fails.
