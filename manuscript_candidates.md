# Manuscript candidate ledger

## Candidate 1 — Selection versus plasticity in residual TNBC

- Question: Are residual programs selected from detectable pretreatment states or induced within persistent clones?
- Novelty: conditional GREEN.
- Evidence: Kim longitudinal scDNA/snRNA; locked tests in Yan and Shiao; subtype control in GSE205472.
- Required analysis: depth-aware nondetection, patient-blocked program change, conservative CNA matching, external transfer.
- Independent validation: mandatory before interpretation.
- Main limitation: sparse destructive sampling and imperfect lineage labels.
- Potential figures: evidence architecture; program reproducibility; within-patient/clone changes; detection-limit analysis; external forest plot; falsification panel.
- Biological significance: clarifies whether residual-state biomarkers imply rare-cell selection, adaptive reprogramming, or both.
- Confidence: low-to-moderate before metadata audit.

- Status: contingent on Candidate 1.
- Question: Does a prespecified macrophage/ECM niche localize the residual program across independent spatial cohorts?
- Limitation: mostly cross-sectional spatial evidence; cannot claim the niche causes entry.
