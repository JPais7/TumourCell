# Phase 4.9.10 — Literature-linked cell clone discovery

Five literature-linked studies were audited, prioritising explicit cell-level clone claims and their public artifacts. No public RNA-barcode→clone mapping survived reconstruction and an independent adversarial audit. SRP114962 remains closest, but only study/sample-level linkage is public; GSE176078, GSE246613 and NEOLETEXE are partial, and GSE205472 is a negative control.

Conclusion: `NO_PUBLIC_REPRODUCIBLE_CELL_CLONE_DATASET_FOUND`, bounded to the audited studies and repositories. The missing observation is a public RNA barcode plus validated malignant identity and clone/genotype assignment in repeated samples. Phase 5 remains locked.
