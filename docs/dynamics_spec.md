# Dynamics specification

The initial target is `p(z[t+1] | z[t], treatment[t])`, using interpretable discrete, Gaussian or probabilistic state-space transitions. Patient-specific counterfactual simulation, neural ODEs, transformers and reinforcement learning are outside the validated scope. The mechanistic representation is `z=(clone, cellular_state, environment)`; without clone/CNV evidence, selection and plasticity are `NOT_IDENTIFIABLE`.
