Assunto: Request for de-identified clinical metadata for GSE319641 / NeoTRIP translational analysis

Dear NeoTRIP Translational Research Team,

I am conducting an independent, outcome-blind validation of pre-specified malignant-cell transcriptional programs in triple-negative breast cancer. We have downloaded the processed, ComBat-adjusted TPM matrix deposited as GSE319641 and have projected the locked signatures without refitting.

The public GEO metadata allow reconstruction of patient ID, baseline/D1C2 timepoint and batch, but do not contain the variables required for the clinical analysis. I would therefore like to request the minimum de-identified metadata necessary to test the pre-specified hypotheses:

- Sample_ID and Patient_ID;
- randomized treatment arm (CT versus CT/A);
- pathological response at surgery (pCR versus residual disease);
- analysis-population indicator, if applicable;
- optional event-free survival time/status, only if approved for sharing;
- definitions and coding dictionary for all supplied fields.

The intended analyses are patient-level and include: baseline association with pCR, paired baseline-to-D1C2 changes, and treatment-arm interaction. No gene selection, signature refitting or threshold optimization will be performed using NeoTRIP outcomes. Results will be reported with uncertainty and multiplicity correction, and the data will be stored and used under any conditions required by the data provider.

Please let me know whether a formal application, data-use agreement or institutional approval is required.

Kind regards,
