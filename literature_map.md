- Wu et al. 2021: 26 breast cancers across subtypes plus spatial transcriptomics; GSE176078 ([Nature Genetics](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41588-021-00911-1)).

## Digital-twin landscape

- Current oncology digital twins remain heterogeneous and often weakly validated ([scoping review](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11593079/)).
- MRI-calibrated breast-cancer digital twins model tumour-scale NAC response, a different scale from cell-state dynamics ([npj Digital Medicine 2025](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41746-025-01579-1)).
- TumorTwin is a modular patient-specific oncology modeling framework, not evidence that breast single-cell transitions are identifiable ([PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42116079/)).
