# Research gaps

## Highest-value gap

No evidence found in this reconnaissance establishes, across independent clinical studies with locked definitions, how much apparent residual-state emergence is attributable to selection of detectable pretreatment states versus within-clone transcriptional change after accounting for nondetection and generic stress.

## Why this remains a gap

- Kim 2018 provides the core selection/reprogramming observation but has a small detailed single-cell cohort.
- Yan 2026 provides power and spatial response association but only pretreatment molecular sampling.
- Immunotherapy cohorts provide serial tissue but different selective pressures.
- RNA-inferred CNAs are imperfect lineage labels.
- Most studies define states and test associations in the same data.

## Non-gaps

The following are already established enough that repeating them is not a central project: TNBC heterogeneity; EMT/hypoxia/stress programs; response-associated immune states; spatial immune niches; and the existence of both genomic selection and transcriptional adaptation.
