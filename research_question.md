# Research question

## Selected question

Across independent TNBC cohorts, do malignant-cell programs enriched in residual disease arise mainly by expansion of detectable pretreatment programs, or by treatment-associated transcriptional reprogramming within persistent CNA-defined clones?

## Primary hypothesis

Both mechanisms occur, but a reproducible residual program increases within persistent clones after chemotherapy and remains detectable after controlling for generic stress, cell cycle, hypoxia, sampling depth, patient, and tumour site.

## Estimands

1. Patient-level change in locked residual-program usage from pretreatment to later biopsy within persistent CNA clone.
2. Fraction of post-treatment program-positive cells compatible with pretreatment presence after depth-aware nondetection correction.
3. Patient-level association between locked pretreatment program burden and residual disease in a fully held-out cohort.

These estimands describe observed changes. They do not identify causal cell trajectories.
